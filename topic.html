PowerBuilding Training

Strength and aesthetics together.

Benefits of PowerBuilding

PowerBuilding improves strength, muscle size and athletic performance. It helps athletes stay balanced between heavy lifting and muscle growth. This style of training also improves confidence, posture and physical endurance. Many people prefer PowerBuilding because it gives both aesthetic and functional results.

Sample Workout

Monday: Bench Press - 5x5 Incline Dumbbell Press - 4x10 Pull-Ups - 4x8 Bicep Curls - 3x12

Wednesday: Squat - 5x5 Romanian Deadlift - 4x8 Leg Press - 4x12 Calf Raises - 4x15
