About Me

Fitness enthusiast and PowerBuilding athlete.

Who Am I?

My name is Boris and I enjoy fitness, strength training and healthy living. I started training to improve my physical strength, confidence and discipline. Over time, the gym became an important part of my lifestyle. PowerBuilding became my favorite training style because it combines muscle growth with real strength. I enjoy challenging myself and constantly improving my performance.

My Goals

My goals are to improve my squat, bench press and deadlift while building a strong physique. I also want to stay healthy, motivated and inspire other people to start working out. Fitness teaches discipline, patience and consistency, which are useful both inside and outside the gym.
