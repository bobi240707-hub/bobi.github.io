What is PowerBuilding?

PowerBuilding is a training style that combines powerlifting and bodybuilding. The goal is to become stronger while also building an aesthetic physique. This training method mixes heavy compound movements with hypertrophy exercises. Many athletes choose PowerBuilding because it develops both strength and muscle size. It is perfect for people who want to look athletic while also improving their overall performance in the gym.

Main Exercises

The main exercises in PowerBuilding are squat, bench press, deadlift, overhead press and pull-ups. These compound exercises train multiple muscle groups and help athletes gain strength much faster. Accessory exercises are also important because they improve muscle definition and endurance.
